PRIVACY FIRST

SOPAN only sets cookies strictly necessary for login authentication and security against website forgery.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites work properly, improve security, and remember your session while navigating pages.

Local Storage: In addition to HTTP cookies, modern web applications use browser `localStorage` to securely store lightweight user preferences (such as your cookie consent choice) directly on your device without sending them over the network with every request.

2. Cookies & Storage Elements Used by SOPAN

Under international privacy regulations (including the EU ePrivacy Directive and global data protection guidelines), strictly necessary cookies do not require opt-in consent because the service cannot function without them. However, we believe in full transparency and list every cookie set by SOPAN below:

Cookie / Token Category Duration Purpose & Security Function
csrftoken Strictly Necessary 1 Year Protects you and the platform against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) attacks by ensuring form submissions originate authentically from your browser session.
sessionid Strictly Necessary 2 Weeks (or until Logout) Stores your encrypted, randomized authentication session token once you log in. Allows you to stay authenticated across candidate dashboards and assessment rooms without re-entering credentials on every page.
sopan_cookie_consent Functional Preference Persistent (`localStorage`) Records your acknowledgment of our Cookie and Privacy policy so that the bottom consent banner is not repetitively displayed on every page load.

3. Zero Advertising & Tracking Cookies

Our Commitment: No Behavioral Surveillance

SOPAN DOES NOT USE third-party advertising cookies, retargeting pixels (such as Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, or Google Remarketing), behavioral trackers, or cross-site fingerprinting beacons. Your activity on our skilling platform is never monetized or shared with ad networks.

4. How You Can Control & Delete Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to manage or delete cookies via browser settings. Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies (`csrftoken` and `sessionid`) will prevent you from signing in or submitting assessments.

  • Google Chrome: Settings ➜ Privacy and Security ➜ Third-party cookies ➜ See all site data and permissions.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings ➜ Privacy & Security ➜ Cookies and Site Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences ➜ Privacy ➜ Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings ➜ Cookies and Site Permissions ➜ Manage and delete cookies.
