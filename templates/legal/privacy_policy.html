2. Personal Data Collected & Data Minimization

We practice strict Data Minimization under Section 4 of the DPDP Act 2023. We collect only what is essential to provide skill assessments, career guidance, and longitudinal employment verification.

Category Specific Data Fields Lawful Purpose

3. Purpose of Data Processing

Your personal data is processed solely for specified, explicit, and lawful purposes:

  • Outcome Measurement: Measuring whether vocational training under PMKVY, NAPS, or institutional programs leads to sustainable jobs and wage growth.
  • Skill Gap Resolution: Comparing candidate verified capabilities with active employer vacancies to recommend targeted upskilling.
  • 1-Click Employer Verification: Enabling employers or HR managers to confirm employment status and wage progression via secure tokenized links.
  • Conversational Support: Powering the SOPAN Rozgar Sahayak bot and AI career guide to provide transparent status summaries.
  • Aggregated Governance Insights: Generating non-identifiable, cohort-level statistical reports for state skill missions and educational institutions.

4. Notice & Consent Architecture (DPDP Act Sec 5 & 6)

In compliance with Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023, consent obtained from you is:

Free & Affirmative You give consent through an active, unchecked opt-in checkbox during sign-up. We never use pre-ticked consent boxes.
Easily Withdrawable You may withdraw your consent at any time from your account settings or by emailing our Grievance Officer, with no retroactive penalty.

If you withdraw consent, we will cease processing your personal data within 7 business days, except where retention is strictly required to comply with statutory legal obligations or audited government reporting mandates.

5. Your Statutory Rights as Data Principal

Under Chapter III (Sections 11 through 14) of the DPDP Act 2023, you have the following enforceable rights:

Right to Access Information (Sec 11)

Obtain a summary of personal data being processed and identities of all entities with whom data has been shared.

Right to Correction & Updating (Sec 12)

Correct inaccurate, incomplete, or out-of-date personal data directly from your profile dashboard.

Right to Erasure / Forgotten (Sec 12)

Request deletion of personal data when the processing purpose is fulfilled or consent is withdrawn.

Right to Nominate (Sec 14)

Designate another individual who may exercise your data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

6. Security & Storage Safeguards (Sec 8 DPDP Act)

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational safeguards:

  • Transport Encryption: All platform traffic is encrypted end-to-end via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Password Cryptography: Passwords are hashed using standard cryptographic algorithms (PBKDF2 with SHA-256) and never stored in readable text.
  • Session Security: Django secure cookie flags (`HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`, CSRF verification) to safeguard against session hijacking.
  • Access Controls: Role-based access controls (RBAC) ensuring candidate records can only be accessed by the candidate or authorized institution administrators.

7. Third Parties, Analytics & Sub-processors

Zero Commercial Data Brokering: We do NOT sell, lease, or monetize your personal information to third-party ad brokers, market researchers, or lead generation networks.

Third-Party Libraries: We utilize trusted Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) including jsDelivr, Cloudflare cdnjs, and Google Fonts solely to deliver CSS styling, icons, and typography. No tracking pixels or behavioral cookies are injected by these resources.

8. Data Retention & Periodic Review

Personal data is retained only for the duration of the longitudinal outcome study (typically up to 24 months post-training completion) or as long as your account remains active. Upon request for deletion or account closure, candidate records are scrubbed within 30 days, leaving only de-identified cohort summary statistics.
