CANDIDATE GUARANTEE

SOPAN never charges candidates or job seekers for taking assessments, viewing skill gaps, or verifying employment.

1. Free Access for Students & Candidates

Zero Cost to Candidates

The SOPAN (सोपान) platform is provided as an open national skilling initiative. Registration, profile creation, skill assessments (MCQ & practical coding), skill gap diagnostics, and longitudinal wage retention logging are 100% free of charge for all individual students, candidates, and job seekers.

Because candidates are never charged any application fees, subscription costs, or assessment fees, no monetary refunds apply to individual candidate accounts.

2. Warning Against Unauthorized Fee Solicitations

Notice to Candidates: Beware of Fraud

SOPAN and its representatives will never ask you for money, bank account details, UPI PINs, or processing fees for job verification, placement assistance, or certificate issuance. If any individual or agency demands payment claiming to represent SOPAN, do not pay and immediately report them to {{ GRIEVANCE_EMAIL|default:"grievance@sopan-career.gov.in" }}.

3. Institutional, Training Partner & Enterprise Terms

Where commercial agreements, institutional cohort benchmarking licenses, or enterprise API services are contracted with educational colleges, corporate employers, or private training partners, the following refund and cancellation terms govern:

  • Cancellation Window: Institutional partners may cancel contracted assessment cohort batches up to 7 calendar days prior to cohort activation for a 100% refund of prepaid fees.
  • Technical Service Interruptions: If an institutional assessment batch cannot be executed due to verified server outages or technical platform failure attributable directly to SOPAN, a full refund or equivalent service credit will be issued within 14 business days.
  • Non-Refundable Scenarios: Once assessment batches have commenced, candidate invitations have been dispatched, or verified cryptographic credential badges have been published on the ledger, the associated service fees are non-refundable.
