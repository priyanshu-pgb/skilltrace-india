How to File a Grievance

If you have any grievance or complaint concerning:

  • Unauthorized processing or disclosure of your personal data;
  • Exercise of your statutory rights (Access, Correction, Erasure, Consent Withdrawal);
  • Discrepancies in verified assessment scores or employer verification records;
  • Suspected security vulnerability or data breach incident;

Please submit a formal notice to our Grievance Officer via email with the subject line format:
[GRIEVANCE - DPDP ACT] - Your Name - Description

Required Information in Your Notice:

  1. Your registered username and primary email address on SOPAN.
  2. Specific nature of the grievance or right being exercised under the DPDP Act 2023.
  3. Any supporting documents or screenshots (if applicable).
  4. Your preferred contact channel for resolution updates.
Statutory Escalation to the Data Protection Board of India

Under Section 13(3) of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, if our Grievance Officer fails to resolve your grievance within 30 days, or if you are dissatisfied with the resolution provided, you may escalate your complaint directly to the Data Protection Board of India.
