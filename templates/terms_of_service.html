SkillTrace India : Terms of Service & Operational Guidelines

Operational Framework for Training Providers, Employers, and State Skill Missions

Effective Date: 1 April 2026
NATIONAL OPERATIONAL STANDARD
1. Purpose and Scope of Platform Institutional Mandate

SkillTrace India is deployed to measure long-term placement, self-employment, wage progression, and retention outcomes across all Centrally Sponsored Skilling Schemes (PMKVY, DDU-GKY, NAPS, ITI Craftsmen Training Scheme, and State Missions).

All entities interacting with this portal (including State Skill Development Missions, Sector Skill Councils, Assessment Agencies, Training Providers, and Employers) agree to abide by these Terms of Service.

2. Obligations of Training Providers (TPs) and Centers (TCs) Reporting Compliance
  • Mandatory Consent Ingestion: Providers must obtain electronic or recorded consent from every enrolled candidate using the authorized standard consent notice prior to data ingestion.
  • Prohibition of Fabricated Placements: Self-reporting of placement without authentic evidence tokens or employer confirmation is strictly prohibited. Submitting false placement documentation constitutes fraud under applicable provisions of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) and will trigger automated de-affiliation and procurement blacklisting.
  • Cooperation with District Verification: Training Providers are legally required to cooperate with District Skill Officers (DSOs) during on-site audits and provide access to contact recovery logs for unreachable cohorts.
3. Employer Verification Protocol Verification Integrity

Employers utilizing the 3-Tap Direct Verification Portal or Bulk GSTIN Integration certify that responses accurately reflect enterprise payroll records. Verified employment reports may be utilized by state labor departments to confirm statutory compliance and apprenticeship subsidy claims.

4. Evidence Tier System and Public Reporting Standard National Transparency

All outcomes published in public reports, Parliament questions, and performance-linked financial releases are strictly graded into Evidence Tiers:

  • Tier A: Statutory Registry match (EPFO / ESIC) or Direct Employer payroll document.
  • Tier B: Employer confirmed via 3-Tap Portal or Provider verified with documentary proof.
  • Tier C: Corroborated candidate self-report.
  • Tier D: Uncorroborated single assertion.
  • Tier U: Unreachable / Attrited after mandatory escalation ladder.

Outcome-linked funding releases are strictly contingent on Evidence Tier A and Tier B verifications.

5. Data Sharing and Open Data Access Anonymization Standard

Public research datasets released through this platform undergo automated cell suppression (k >= 10) and differential privacy noise. Direct queries exposing individual trainee attributes or non-consented contact points are blocked by access-control filters.
