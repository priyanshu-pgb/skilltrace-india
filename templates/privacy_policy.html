SkillTrace India : Privacy Policy & Data Protection Charter

Enacted in Full Compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP)

Notice Version: v2.1-DPDP2023
STATUTORY COMPLIANCE
1. Statutory Framework and Lawful Grounds Section 4 & Section 6 DPDP Act 2023

SkillTrace India is a national digital public infrastructure operated by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE). The platform processes personal data of vocational trainees exclusively for specified, legitimate, and consented purposes under Section 4 and Section 6 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.

Consent is obtained at the point of enrolment in clear, plain language across 12 scheduled Indian languages. Consent artefacts are stored as cryptographically signed electronic records with verifiable timestamp hashes.

2. Specified Purposes of Data Processing Purpose Limitation

Personal data is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • Follow-up contact at T+30, T+90, T+180, T+365, and T+730 days to measure employment, wage bands, and retention.
  • Confirmation of employment status with prospective and confirmed hiring employers.
  • Cross-linkage with statutory registries (EPFO, ESIC, NAPS) via hashed anonymized matching protocols.
  • Referral to job matching opportunities and apprenticeship counseling for unplaced trainees.
  • De-identified statistical research and national curriculum improvement analytics.
3. Architecture of Data Minimisation and Identity Vault Separation Privacy by Design

To prevent surveillance or misuse, SkillTrace India implements architectural separation:

  • Zero Raw Aadhaar Storage: Raw Aadhaar numbers are never collected or stored. Identification relies on non-derivable SkillTrace Identifiers (STID) and HMAC-SHA256 salted contact hashes.
  • Separate Identity Vault: Personally Identifiable Information (name, date of birth, contact details) resides in a logically separated, access-controlled vault database. Analytics engines access only pseudonymized STIDs.
  • k-Anonymity Guard: Public and inter-departmental analytics enforce a strict threshold (k >= 10). Cells with fewer than 10 individuals are suppressed.
4. Rights of Data Principals Section 11, 12, 13 DPDP Act 2023

Every trainee possesses irrevocable statutory rights under Indian law:

  • Right to Access: View all recorded outcome episodes, evidence documents, and registered contact points.
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate employment status, wage bands, or demographic attributes.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke consent at any time via SMS, WhatsApp bot, or web desk. Contact attempts cease within 24 hours of revocation.
  • Right to Erasure: Request permanent anonymization of personal identifiers upon completion of scheme tracking periods.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: File complaints directly to the Data Protection Officer or escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.
