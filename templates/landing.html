The SkillBridge Skilling Lifecycle

Bridging the gap between training and employment requires an end-to-end evidence pipeline.

1

Claimed Skill

Declare candidate abilities and upload existing certifications as unverified baselines.

2

Assess

Timed randomized MCQs with anti-cheating logic and practical coding challenges.

3

Verify

Weighted server-side computation (MCQ 20%, Practical 50%, Project 30%) yielding verified scores.

4

Compare with Job

Benchmark individual skill ratings against active industry role requirements.

5

Identify Gap

Calculate exact numeric deltas (Required Level minus Candidate Level) per required skill.

6

Recommend Training

Direct enrollment into curriculum mapped directly to diagnosed skill deficiencies.

7

Re-Assess

Post-training re-evaluation to quantify and visualize exact before vs after improvement delta.

8

Track & Analyze

Longitudinal employment verification and institutional skilling ROI analytics.
